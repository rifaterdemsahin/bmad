## Content Strategy

### The 3-Post Cycle

| Post | Purpose | CTA |
|------|---------|-----|
| **1. Essay** (Mon) | Big idea + story | "See the live model →" |
| **2. Thread** (Wed) | 5-tweet breakdown of essay | "Full architecture here →" |
| **3. Update** (Fri) | What we shipped this week | "Track progress on our kanban →" |

### Linking to the BMAD Site

Every post must include **one** of these CTAs:

- 🔗 *"Explore the live BMAD architecture dashboard → [link]"*
- 🔗 *"Track our open kanban board → [link]"*
- 🔗 *"Run the ROI model yourself → [link]"*
- 🔗 *"Meet the BMAD agents (Analyst, Architect, Orchestrator) → [link]"*

## Growth Tactics

### Phase 1: Foundation (Weeks 1–4)
- [ ] Publish 4 essays (2 Chidi, 2 Rifat)
- [ ] Cross-post to LinkedIn with `#BMADMethod` hashtag
- [ ] Add BMAD site link to both LinkedIn bios
- [ ] Create Twitter/X threads summarizing each essay

### Phase 2: Collaboration (Weeks 5–8)
- [ ] Guest-post on 3 relevant AI/engineering newsletters
- [ ] Invite 1 external expert per month for a joint essay
- [ ] Host first virtual "BMAD Office Hours" (Zoom + Substack Live)

### Phase 3: Monetization (Weeks 9–12)
- [ ] Launch paid tier: "BMAD Inner Circle"
- [ ] Paid perk: Early access to `bmad-in-a-box` releases
- [ ] Paid perk: Monthly 1:1 office hours with Chidi or Rifat
